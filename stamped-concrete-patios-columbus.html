How the work gets done

Tools & Methods Behind Summit Concrete Patios's Stamped Concrete Patios Ohio

We use a mix of solid tools and proven techniques to get stamped concrete patios right. From smoothing wet concrete to imprinting patterns, each step demands the right gear and know-how. This keeps your patio durable and beautiful through Ohio’s ups and downs.

The equipment we use

🧰

Concrete Mixer for Consistent Batches

We use a heavy-duty concrete mixer to blend the right mix of cement, sand, and water. Getting this balance is key for a patio that won’t crack or crumble over time.

🧰

Bull Float for a Smooth Surface

The bull float levels and smooths fresh concrete before it sets. It removes ridges and air pockets, creating a flat base that’s perfect for stamping.

🧰

Stamping Mats for Pattern Impressions

We press rubber stamping mats into wet concrete to create textures that mimic stone, brick, or slate. This step defines the patio’s look and adds grip underfoot.

🧰

Edging Tools for Clean Borders

Edging tools shape the patio’s edges while the concrete is still soft, preventing cracks and giving a crisp finish where the patio meets grass or landscaping.

🧰

Color Hardener Application

We broadcast color hardeners over the surface to add depth and durability. This helps the patio resist stains and fading from sun and weather.

The methods behind it

📐

Release Agents to Prevent Sticking

Before stamping, we spray release agents on the surface to stop the stamping mats from sticking to the concrete. This keeps the patterns clean and sharp.

📐

Curing Blankets for Even Drying

We use curing blankets to cover the patio after finishing. They hold in moisture and heat, letting the concrete cure slowly and evenly to avoid cracks.

📐

Sealing for Protection and Shine

Once the concrete cures, we apply a sealant to lock in color and protect against water and stains. This step keeps your patio looking fresh and lasts longer.

📐

Control Joint Cutting to Prevent Cracks

We cut control joints into the patio after it sets to guide where cracks form. This keeps cracks from ruining the look or structure.

📐

Proper Site Preparation and Subgrade Compaction

We make sure the ground beneath your patio is compacted and level. This foundation prevents settling that can lead to cracks or uneven surfaces.
